CARES

Conversational Audio Reaction Scenes

Two people talk. A sound happens. What the conversation does with that sound — seize it, brush it off, stumble over it, ignore it — is the label. The sound is never named. Recovering the label means hearing the sound and reading what the dialogue makes of it.

Code on GitHub Listen to five scenes Marcel Gibier

Four ways to react

Every sound placed in a conversation carries exactly one of these, decided before the dialogue is written. The writer has to realise it; the listener has to recover it.

Pivot
The sound takes over. It becomes the subject for several turns.
Verbal
One speaker acknowledges it in passing, then the conversation resumes.
Behavioral
Nobody mentions it, but it visibly disrupts the speaking.
Ambient
Nobody reacts at all.

Five scenes

Full mixes, as they ship. Each one is about a minute and a half; the sound arrives at the time given.

Pivot

beach
watch_alarm · 48s
1 min 35

AIn my notes. On my phone. Which is with me at all times, so.

a watch alarm goes off

BHang on, was that you? Something just went off right by me.

The sound becomes the subject. Everything that was being argued about stops.

Verbal

café
chair_scraping · 72s
1 min 30

A…I've told Marco he's on potatoes and he's furious about the demotion.

a chair scrapes

BOof, that went right through my back teeth. Sharp. Anyway, poor Marco, twenty years of stuffing rights gone in one phone call.

Noted, then dropped. The word anyway is doing the work — the subject survives.

Behavioral

airport
security_conveyor_belt · 16s
1 min 32

AManchester. Back Sunday. Actually, funny thing, I've got the Pixies next Friday. Dan sorted it.

a security conveyor belt starts

BWait, next Friday, at the… the big one, the one by the station, it's got the ugly roof, I can never… anyway, yeah, that place.

Never mentioned. It shows only in the way the sentence comes apart and is rebuilt.

Ambient

public park
baby_crying · 20s
1 min 33

AShe sits out on that balcony with her tea and she's already got a list of things to tell me.

a baby starts crying

BA list. Of course she does. And you just let her run through it?

Clearly audible, and the conversation does not bend around it. This is the hard negative: a sound that is there and means nothing.

Pivot

kitchen
siren_ambulance · 33s
rare sound
1 min 39

AHe keeps saying he's just being direct. Which, fine, be direct in the huddle. Not while I'm trying to read a hitter.

an ambulance siren passes

BWhoa, that's right outside. That was RIGHT outside!

Rare sounds are always pivots — the one case where the sound outranks whatever the conversation was about.
